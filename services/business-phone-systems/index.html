Common questions

Before we start.

Can we keep our existing phone numbers?

Numbers can commonly be ported, but the provider, account information, timing, and number status must be reviewed before a migration plan is finalized.

Do we need desk phones?

Not always. Many systems support a mix of desk phones, computer apps, and mobile apps. The right combination depends on roles and workflow.

Does VoIP depend on our network?

Yes. Internet reliability, internal network quality, power, and configuration all affect voice performance, which is why phone and network planning should be coordinated.
