Where we help

Common questions

Before we start.

Do you replace our existing IT provider?

Not necessarily. StrataWorks can coordinate with current vendors, fill a strategy gap, or handle a defined project without disrupting relationships that already work.

Is this only for businesses with an IT department?

No. This is especially useful for owners and managers who need senior-level guidance but do not need a full-time internal technology leader.

Can you work remotely?

Yes. Planning, vendor coordination, cloud work, automation, and documentation can often be handled remotely; onsite work is available in the South Carolina Midlands.
