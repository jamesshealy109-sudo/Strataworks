07 / Before we talk

Useful answers.
No sales fog.

What are managed IT services?

Managed IT services give your business defined, ongoing responsibility for technology support and upkeep. That can include troubleshooting, network and firewall management, vendor coordination, documentation, and improvement planning. We agree on the systems and responsibilities in scope before work begins.

How much does managed IT support cost for a small business?

Scope varies by users, locations, systems, responsibilities, and support requirements. A business with one office and a few cloud applications has different needs from a company with servers, multiple sites, and customer-facing systems. The consultation helps establish the work needed before a proposal is prepared.

Do you provide onsite IT support in Columbia and Lexington?

Yes. StrataWorks provides onsite support in Columbia, Lexington, and nearby South Carolina Midlands communities, with remote work where appropriate. We discuss location, scope, and scheduling as part of the engagement.

Can StrataWorks work with our existing IT vendors?

Yes. We can coordinate with internet, software, telecom, and other technology providers. The goal is clear ownership across the environment, including where one vendor’s responsibility meets another’s.

Can you manage our business network and firewall?

Yes. We can assess and manage switching, routing, Wi-Fi, segmentation, firewall configuration, access, and updates. We review the equipment and business requirements before defining the scope.

Can you help with backup internet and business continuity?

Yes. We can review secondary connections, failover, cellular or alternate connectivity, backups, and recovery planning. Options depend on the location and systems involved; the plan should account for the services your business needs during an outage.

Do you support business phone systems?

Yes. StrataWorks helps with VoIP, call routing, mobile access, migrations, and carrier coordination. We also consider network readiness and continuity because the phone system depends on more than the handset.

Can you work alongside an internal IT person or department?

Yes. We can support defined projects, infrastructure work, vendor coordination, or senior technical planning alongside your internal team. Responsibilities and handoffs are agreed so the engagement adds capacity without creating confusion.
