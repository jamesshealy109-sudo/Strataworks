07 / Before we talk

Useful answers.
No sales fog.

Is our business too small for managed IT?

No. A five-person team or a newly opening business can need reliable IT just as much as a larger office. We look at what you depend on and what you want someone else to manage, not just the employee count.

What does ongoing managed IT support include?

The agreed scope can include user support, Microsoft 365 administration, computers, networks, backups, security basics, and vendor coordination. We document the responsibilities so you know what is covered.

How much does small business IT support cost?

Pricing depends on users, devices, locations, systems, and support responsibilities. We review the environment and propose an appropriate support plan. Assessment, project work, and ongoing support have agreed scope and fees; they are not automatically included for free.

Can you help with a new employee or someone leaving?

Yes. We can manage Microsoft 365 accounts, licensing, device setup, and access changes as part of an agreed onboarding and offboarding process.

Do you provide local and remote support?

Yes. We provide remote support and onsite assistance where appropriate in Lexington, Columbia, and the Midlands. Location, scheduling, and responsibilities are discussed when defining the engagement.

Can you work with our vendors or internal IT person?

Yes. We can coordinate internet, software, printer, and phone vendors, or take on agreed responsibilities alongside your internal IT team.

Can you help us open another location?

Yes. We can plan devices, business Wi-Fi, connectivity, phones, vendors, and documentation for a new office or location. We agree the project scope before implementation.
