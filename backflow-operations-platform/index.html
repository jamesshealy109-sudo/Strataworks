06 / Common questions

Before the demo.

What is backflow testing software?

It is software built around the records and work of a backflow testing business: customers, locations, assemblies, tests, schedules, reports, and billing. StrataWorks connects these in one operations platform.

Can this replace spreadsheets and generic field-service tools?

The platform brings backflow records and workflows together so teams can move away from spreadsheet duplication and disconnected tools. Your demo is a chance to compare your current process with the platform and identify what fits.

Does it handle backflow test forms?

Yes. The platform supports test reporting and municipality-specific forms where the required jurisdiction mappings are configured. We can review your form requirements during the demo.

Can technicians use it in the field?

Yes. Technician workflows support field testing and follow-up work, with office and field roles connected to the operational record.

Does it handle scheduling and route planning?

Yes. Scheduling, technician assignments, and route planning are part of the platform, alongside customer, location, and assembly records.

Can it handle invoices and payments?

Yes. The platform includes invoices, receivables, and payment workflows so billing and follow-up stay connected to the work.

Does it support municipality submission workflows?

Yes, where configured. Municipality-specific form/submission workflows depend on configured jurisdiction requirements. Bring the jurisdictions you work with so we can review the fit.

Is it available outside South Carolina?

Yes. It is available nationwide with a strong focus on South Carolina, North Carolina, and Georgia. Municipality requirements still need to be reviewed for your service area.

Can data be imported from another system?

A Jobber customer CSV import workflow is verified. If you use a different system or need additional records moved, we can review the export and scope the migration; other automated imports are not promised.
