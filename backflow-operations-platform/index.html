The entire job.
One connected record.

Backflow work is more than filling out a report. See how a customer, an assembly, a field test, an approved form, and an invoice stay connected from start to finish.

  1. Plan
  2. Test
  3. Review
  4. Invoice
  5. Submit

  1. 1 Plan the work and the route

    Keep customers, service locations, and assemblies together. Schedule the test, assign a technician and gauge, and put the stop on a route.

    The field team knows where to go and which device needs testing.

  2. 2 Record the test in the field

    Open assigned work on a phone, enter readings against the right assembly, add notes or photos when needed, and save a draft before sending it for office review.

    Readings and context reach the office with the job.

  3. 3 Turn readings into a reviewed report

    Office staff review the submitted test, check the configured authority form, and approve the resulting PDF. The customer, assembly, tester, and readings remain tied to the record.

    Less copying the same readings onto another form.

  4. 4 Invoice the work and see what is owed

    Create an invoice linked to the customer, location, and approved test. Issue it, track its balance, and record payment without losing the path back to the completed job.

    The office can see the work behind every balance.

  5. 5 Prepare and send the approved form

    Eligible reports move through a municipality submission queue. After the required review and payment checks, approved reports can be emailed through your configured submission workflow, with delivery history kept alongside the job.

    A sent email is visible; the authority's acceptance still needs confirmation.

How does the 60-month rate guarantee work?

The $300 monthly base subscription rate stays the same for 60 months from signup while your subscription remains continuously active. The guarantee ends if you cancel. Applicable tax and separately scoped services are additional.

Does the platform submit the report for me?

Yes. Eligible approved reports are sent through your configured email submission workflow, with the report and submission history kept with the job. Approval, payment rules, a completed report, and a configured recipient determine when a report can be sent. Sending does not confirm acceptance by the authority.

Can I add my own municipalities and custom forms?

Yes. Teams nationwide can add municipalities and custom forms through the app, even when a form is not in our supplied library. Form fields need to be mapped and checked before live use. Confirm the current form, recipient, and accepted submission method with the authority. Read the submission guide.

What if an authority requires a portal?

The submission workflow described here is email. If a provider requires a portal or another method, check that requirement with us before subscribing. Ask about your workflow.

Can I bring data from another system?

A Jobber customer CSV import workflow is verified. Tell us which records you want to move. Other sources and more extensive migrations need their own agreed scope.

How do invoices and payments work?

The platform keeps invoices, receivables, and payment history with the work. Optional payment-provider connections are configured for your business.
