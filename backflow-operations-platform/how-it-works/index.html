Backflow Operations / How it works

The entire job.
One connected record.

Backflow work is more than filling out a report. See how a customer, an assembly, a field test, an approved form, and an invoice stay connected from start to finish.

  1. Plan
  2. Test
  3. Review
  4. Invoice
  5. Submit

  1. 1 Plan the work and the route

    Keep customers, service locations, and assemblies together. Schedule the test, assign a technician and gauge, and put the stop on a route.

    The field team knows where to go and which device needs testing.

  2. 2 Record the test in the field

    Open assigned work on a phone, enter readings against the right assembly, add notes or photos when needed, and save a draft before sending it for office review.

    Readings and context reach the office with the job.

  3. 3 Turn readings into a reviewed report

    Office staff review the submitted test, check the configured authority form, and approve the resulting PDF. The customer, assembly, tester, and readings remain tied to the record.

    Less copying the same readings onto another form.

  4. 4 Invoice the work and see what is owed

    Create an invoice linked to the customer, location, and approved test. Issue it, track its balance, and record payment without losing the path back to the completed job.

    The office can see the work behind every balance.

  5. 5 Prepare and send the approved form

    Eligible reports move through a municipality submission queue. After the required review and payment checks, approved reports can be emailed through your configured submission workflow, with delivery history kept alongside the job.

    A sent email is visible; the authority's acceptance still needs confirmation.
